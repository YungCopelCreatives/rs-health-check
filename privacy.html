Privacy Policy

RS Practice Health Check™ · Last updated 23 November 2026

This policy explains how RS Medical Consultancy (Pty) Ltd ("RS", "we") collects and uses personal information through the RS Practice Health Check™, which runs on WhatsApp and on this website. We process personal information in line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

What we collect

  • Your name, practice name and medical speciality.
  • Your WhatsApp number and WhatsApp profile name, and your email address if you use the web form.
  • Your answers to the 13 Health Check statements, your biggest practice challenge if you choose to share it, and your practice's approximate annual turnover band.
  • Messages you send to our WhatsApp number.

We do not collect patient information. Please do not share any patient details in the Health Check or in messages to us.

How we use it

  • To calculate and show your Practice Health Score.
  • To prepare for and arrange your complimentary consultation, and to send your RS Practice Health Report™.
  • To contact you about your results on WhatsApp or email.

Your turnover band is used internally to prepare for your consultation and does not affect your score. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who processes it

We use trusted service providers to run the Health Check: Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform), Vercel (hosting), Upstash (temporary chat progress), Make (automation), Airtable (our client records) and Calendly (booking). Some of these providers store data outside South Africa, under their own security and privacy commitments.

How long we keep it

Unfinished Health Check progress is deleted automatically after 14 days. Completed results and contact details are kept for as long as needed to provide our services to you, or until you ask us to delete them.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, or object to us contacting you. Reply STOP on WhatsApp at any time to stop messages from us.

Deleting your data

To have your information deleted, email rsconsultadmin@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Health Check data" and the WhatsApp number you used. We will delete your records within 30 days and confirm by email.

Contact

RS Medical Consultancy (Pty) Ltd, Cape Town, South Africa. Email rsconsultadmin@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa.
